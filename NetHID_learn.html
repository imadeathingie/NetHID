Learn a remote

Capture IR or 433 MHz codes from your own remotes, replay them, and save them in this browser.

Point the source remote at the receiver, click Learn, then press a button. IR uses the TSOP receiver (GPIO18); RF uses the 433 MHz receiver (GPIO19). RF is noisier — it may take a couple of presses. Captured codes replay through this NetHID's IR LED / 433 transmitter.
